About me

I'm an applied physicist and researcher at Fondazione Eucentre in Pavia, where I develop software and apply AI and machine learning to earthquake engineering, with a focus on computer vision. Before that, my academic work spanned Raman and SERS spectroscopy, machine learning on experimental data and nuclear physics — plus two years teaching physics in high schools.
